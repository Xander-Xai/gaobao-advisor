# Community code of conduct

## Our standard

We want a respectful, technically rigorous community where people can ask
questions and contribute regardless of experience, background or identity.

Expected behaviour includes constructive review, clear evidence, respect for
privacy, attribution of others' work, and willingness to correct mistakes.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual
attention, publishing private information, sharing credentials, impersonating
an official education service, or pressuring maintainers to provide personal
admission decisions.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions and other
spaces officially operated by the project. It also applies when someone
publicly represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainer contact or,
when the report also involves a vulnerability or private data, through
`SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions,
reject contributions, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, repeated behaviour and
the safety of affected people. Retaliation against a reporter is unacceptable.
