# Support policy

## Community support

Use GitHub Discussions, when enabled, for usage questions and GitHub Issues for
reproducible software defects. Search existing reports first and include the
software version, deployment method and minimal synthetic reproduction.

The project is maintained on a best-effort basis. There is no guaranteed
response time, uptime, data freshness or compatibility with every model or data
provider.

## Not provided

Community maintainers do not provide:

- individualized admission, legal or financial advice;
- guarantees of admission outcomes, policy accuracy or data freshness;
- support for leaked credentials or unauthorized third-party data collection;
- debugging of private deployments without a minimal sanitized reproduction;
- emergency production operations or a service-level agreement.

Always verify admission policies, scores and enrollment plans against the
official provincial examination authority and university notices.

## Security and privacy

Do not use a public issue for vulnerabilities or private information. Follow
`SECURITY.md`. Before sharing logs or screenshots, remove credentials, session
identifiers, conversations and applicant details.
